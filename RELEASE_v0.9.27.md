# v0.9.27

Auto simplify uses direct dictionary synonym scores and the global reader struggle threshold, with no curated whitelist, phrase substitutions, history gate, or graded vocabulary restriction. The strongest known direct alternative wins; ties prefer mastery then lexical order. Terms, noncanonical surface forms and manually restored occurrences remain unchanged. The preview bounds are the minimum and maximum of winning alternatives in the selected chapter edition. Threshold changes clamp the preferred acceptance score to the available range. Apply commits both settings; closing discards the draft. Synonym scores do not guarantee contextual equivalence.

Particle Zoo expands from 72 to 96 exercises, including 啊, 嘛, potential complements and ten additional classifiers. Existing IDs and learning records are unchanged. All choices and base tokens have master dictionary coverage.

Future grammar goal: every vocabulary headword should appear as an actual grammar sentence token (not merely a distractor). Baseline: 5,664 of 13,391 headwords covered; 7,727 remain. No grammar changes in this release.
